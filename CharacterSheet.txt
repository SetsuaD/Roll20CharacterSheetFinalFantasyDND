Final Fantasy–Style D&D Party Sheets

Roll method used: 4d6, drop the lowest die, assign the remaining 3d6 total to stats.

Party concept: A balanced FF1–FF6 inspired “A Team” built around classic roles: Paladin/Knight, White Mage, Thief, and Black Mage/Summoner.

Faction setup:

Faction 1 — Kingdom of Cornelia: Cecil Highwind and Lenna Highwind, siblings.
Faction 2 — Kingdom of Figaro: Locke Valeclaw and Terra Figaro, childhood friends.
The kingdoms are separate but not enemies. This party is a joint envoy/adventuring team sent to investigate crystal disturbances, monster raids, and lost ancient magic.
